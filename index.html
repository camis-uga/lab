GRIP-Fusion: Roadside Camera–LiDAR Perception for Intersection Safety

Sponsored by GDOT, this project collects a new roadside intersection dataset with LiDAR, camera and traffic-conflict labels, and develops GRIP-Fusion, a one-LiDAR + one-camera 3D detector that achieves state-of-the-art results on three public benchmarks.

EgoManeuver: Fine-Grained Ego-Vehicle Action Recognition

A benchmark that unifies seven public driving datasets (86.6K segments, 190.8 hours of video) into 16 lateral and 6 longitudinal ego-vehicle actions, with the DenseEgo baseline and an agent that retrieves driving video by maneuver.

PACE-ADS: Psychology and Cognition Enabled Automated Driving

A framework that personalizes automated driving to the occupant: a driver agent reads the traffic scene, a psychologist agent infers occupant state from EEG, heart rate, facial expression and verbal commands, and a coordinator selects and explains the driving behavior, tested in four closed-loop CARLA scenarios.

Probing LLMs for Autonomous Driving Behavior

A study of how GPT-4o, DeepSeek-V3 and Llama 3.1 choose high-level driving actions in four everyday scenarios, each with 1,500 contextual variants in three prompt languages.

Sample-and-Verify: Test-Time Plan Selection for Vision-Language Driving Models

Sample-and-verify keeps a fine-tuned vision-language planner frozen, adds temperature samples to its greedy plan, and lets a small verifier trained only on logged futures select the plan to execute.

StuckSolver: LLM-Assisted Recovery of Immobilized Autonomous Vehicles

A plug-in LLM agent that detects when an autonomous vehicle is stuck, reasons about the cause from camera images and perception data, and issues a recovery plan on its own or with passenger guidance.
